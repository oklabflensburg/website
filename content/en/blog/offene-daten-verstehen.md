## The question comes before the dataset

What do you want to understand? Where is playground information missing, or which facilities are nearby? A specific question helps you find suitable data.

## Check three things

Record the source, licence and freshness. A publicly available download is not automatically free to reuse. Check whether fields are explained and which values are missing.

## Start small

Inspect a few records and compare them with a place you know. Document uncertainty. A reproducible data check can be a valuable first contribution.
