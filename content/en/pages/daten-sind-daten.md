## The association and the lab

DatenSindDaten e.V. is the supporting association of OK Lab Flensburg. The lab is where people learn and work on projects together; the association provides the organisational framework.

## What support makes possible

Open data, free software and shared infrastructure need dependable organisation. The association supports this framework and enables work on shared tools.

## Help sustain the work

Would you like to contribute infrastructure, time or knowledge? [Contact us](/kontakt). Membership, funding, sponsorship and donation options will be published once their terms are confirmed.
