## Learn together

Treat others respectfully regardless of background, gender, disability, religion, age or experience. Ask rather than assume knowledge. Critique ideas and work, not people.

## Respect boundaries

Harassment, discrimination, threats and publishing private information have no place here. Ask for consent before taking photos or publishing personal details. Respect a no.

## Find support

If a problem arises, speak to an organiser or use the general contact address. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. Do not report personal incidents in public issues.

This code is a proposal for community discussion. Confidential contacts and the process for reports and actions will be published once they have been agreed together.
