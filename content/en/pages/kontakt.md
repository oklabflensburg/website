## Write to us

Use our general contact address for questions about projects, the lab or the association. Briefly describe your topic so we can direct your enquiry.

## Bugs and suggestions

Technical feedback is also welcome as an issue in the relevant project repository. Please do not publish personal or confidential information there.

## Meet in person

An open meetup is a good opportunity for a first conversation. No prior experience is required.
